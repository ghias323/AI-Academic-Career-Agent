## 📌 Overview

**AI-Academic-Career-Agent** is an intelligent autonomous assistant designed specifically for Computer Science researchers, candidates, and students. 

Instead of spending dozens of hours manually searching forums and job boards, this agent automatically scours top global university websites, academic portals, and scholarship platforms in real-time. It discovers, analyzes, and tracks **fully funded PhD programs** and **CS research/faculty roles** matching your exact profile—so you never miss a life-changing opportunity.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🌐 Global Coverage:** Automatically indexes opportunities from top university portals and scholarship boards worldwide.
* **💰 Funding-First Search:** Prioritizes fully funded PhD positions, research fellowships, and faculty roles.
* **🤖 Smart Filtering:** Analyzes job descriptions and criteria to match your specific CS sub-field and interests.
* **⚡ Continuous Tracking:** Monitors portals in real-time so you get updated on new postings immediately.
